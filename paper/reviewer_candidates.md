## Recommended final composition

Do **not** simply choose the five closest prior-art authors. Prefer a balanced slate such as:

1. one graph-evaluation / degree-bias expert;
2. one network-biology bias expert;
3. one biomedical KG/generalization expert;
4. one temporal hypothesis-discovery expert;
5. one broad biological-ML auditing / AI-for-science expert.

This reduces the risk that the paper is judged as only a specialist link-prediction benchmark while still ensuring methodological rigor.

## Exclusion rule

Reviewer exclusion must be based on a concrete conflict or confidentiality/competitive reason, not on the fact that a researcher published close prior art or might be demanding. A skeptical but independent domain expert is preferable to a friendly but scientifically peripheral reviewer.
