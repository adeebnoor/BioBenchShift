# Named later-supported BioGRID case-study amendment — 13 September 2026

## Status and purpose

This amendment is committed after the aggregate frozen BioGRID later-evidence counts are known but **before the identities of the later-supported Top-100 pairs are inspected for case-study selection**. It does not change the frozen BioGRID endpoint. Its purpose is to expose, without hand-picking, the concrete biological interactions underlying the already-reported aggregate result.

Frozen baseline: `1298e4c83ff4fa488a784d52219d20e706118a9e`.

## Mechanical selection rule

Use exactly the same historical BioGRID MV-Physical release 5.0.250, later release 5.0.261, historical node universe, complete historical non-edge candidate universe, model fitting rules and ensemble definitions used by the frozen BioGRID later-evidence analysis.

1. Reconstruct the frozen full-universe SVD and NeuralMF rankings without opening any additional evidence source.
2. Identify every pair in the **SVD Top-100** that belongs to the frozen set of 5,635 later-added closed-world interactions. The frozen aggregate result states that this set contains seven pairs; all seven must be retained.
3. For each selected pair, report its SVD rank, NeuralMF rank, and LightGCN rank in the identical frozen candidate universe.
4. Do not substitute, remove or reorder pairs on the basis of perceived biological importance.
5. Also report the reciprocal control: every later-added interaction in the NeuralMF Top-100. The frozen aggregate count is zero; if reconstruction disagrees, stop and treat this as a reproducibility failure.

## Annotation rule

After pair identities are fixed by the rule above, annotate both proteins using deterministic public identifiers already present in BioGRID and/or UniProt. Extract the later BioGRID record(s) supporting each pair from release 5.0.261 and report available publication identifiers and experimental-system metadata. Annotation cannot change membership in the seven-pair case-study set.

## Interpretation boundary

These named examples are **later-supported interactions**, not prospective wet-lab discoveries generated by this study. Later BioGRID additions reflect subsequent research and curation. The case-study table may demonstrate that benchmark-selected queues would have placed concrete subsequently recorded biological interactions at very different ranks, but it cannot claim that the model caused their discovery, that unrecorded pairs are negatives, or that SVD is universally superior.
